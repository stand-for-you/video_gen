# Scroll Kaos Studio

Makes a finished **Scroll Kaos** paper-cutout horror video set from a story, the same way every time:

- YouTube video, 1920x1080, with chapter cards and a 20-second end screen
- Reel, 1080x1920, drawn again for vertical (not resized), with burned-in captions and ONE narrator voice
- English and Hindi subtitles (.srt)
- 3 YouTube thumbnails for an A/B test (1280x720) and 1 Reel thumbnail (1080x1920)
- One upload-details file in your approved layout (the "It's Inside Your House" format): titles, A/B test, description with chapters, tags, pinned comment, Shorts, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest, the community post plan, upload order and flags

## How to use
Attach or paste a story and say **/scroll-kaos-video** (or "make the Scroll Kaos video", or "reel only"). Claude works automatically, checks every picture and number, and sends the files at the end.

## What is inside
- `skills/scroll-kaos-video/SKILL.md` - the workflow Claude follows
- `skills/scroll-kaos-video/references/` - the full channel rules, the exact pipeline, the art library, the list of look kits already used, and your approved upload .md (gold format) with its guide
- `skills/scroll-kaos-video/engine/` - the complete working project: the permanent cast (Noah Reed, the three ghosts, the side cast), prop libraries from earlier videos, voice, shot builder, sound mix, loudness, subtitles, safe audio, render, thumbnail and QA scripts, and the full Smile Dog story as a worked example

## Requirements
Runs in a Claude session that has a Linux workspace with Node.js, Python 3, ffmpeg and internet access to npm, PyPI and GitHub (the setup script downloads the Kokoro voice model, about 340 MB, and the Lora font). Two CPU cores are enough; a 12-minute video takes about 1.5-2 hours to render.

## What changed in v0.4.0
- **Quality law** (`references/quality-law.md`): smooth motion (every shot moves, no long freezes), no repeated pictures, full-body monsters drawn from the story text, face-only framing limited to 15 % of shots, readable rooms with textured sheets and blankets, and clear blood when the story has blood.
- **Quality gate** (`engine/tools/quality_gate.py`): measures size, frame rate, shots per minute, repeated shots and freezes on the finished videos. Run it on both videos before sending.
- **Workflow**: new planning files `story/creature.md` and `story/rooms.md`, framing and motion tags in the scene tables, and the gate in the final QA step.
- **Known limit**: the gate measures rhythm and repetition, not the drawing itself. Look checks (monster, rooms, blood, framing) still need eyes on the contact sheets.
- **Open policy question (UNVERIFIED)**: the earlier channel policy said "no blood". v0.4 asks for clear blood when the story has it. Confirm with the channel owner before the next video.
