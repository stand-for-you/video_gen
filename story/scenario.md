# "Eyeless Jack" - Scroll Kaos 3D scenario (engine v0.5, kit r)

## 1. Source and rights
- Retold from the creepypasta "Eyeless Jack", posted to Creepypasta by the user **Azelf5000** on **25 February 2012**; the page the
  user gave also says the author later **removed** the original story himself. UNVERIFIED: who holds the rights now, and whether
  narration permission can be given at all. The video is a **retelling in our own words** with original 3D animation.
- The famous photograph of the blue mask was made on **19 November 2009** by two DeviantArt users (nickteezy408 and Pirate-Cashoo)
  and is NOT ours to copy. Our creature is an **original design** built only from the words of the story: a hood, a blank face,
  two hollow sockets, dark fluid. The mask is dark grey-green, not the famous blue, and no photograph is reproduced.

## 2. Softened (policy 2E / 1)
- The organ taking is **never shown**: no wound, no blood on skin, no surgery, no eating. The hospital scene shows a covered
  shoulder, a doctor's hands and a chart; the words carry the rest.
- The brother's body in the hallway is shown only as a **dark shape far down a dark corridor**, out of focus, no detail, no red.
- The thing beside him (the taken kidney) is shown as a **small dark bundle in a cloth**, never opened on camera.
- No weapon ever touches a person on screen. No gore anywhere. No flashing: the camera flash is one slow white fade (0.4 s).

## 3. Story bible
- **Mitch** (hero, our Noah Reed) - early thirties, lost his house, moves in with his brother.
- **Edwin** (NEW cast spec) - the older brother, warm, untidy hair, grey work shirt. Dies on the last night.
- **The doctor** (Elias Gray with a white coat over his clothes - a story reason) - calm, careful, says the hard sentences.
- **The Eyeless One** (NEW creature spec `ej`) - about 1.95 m, thin, **black hoodie with the hood up**, a blank dark grey-green
  face with **no nose and no mouth**, **two hollow black sockets**, **dark fluid running from the sockets**, long thin fingers,
  moves in a crouch, leans over a sleeping person, breathes, its fingers curl.
- **Places**: Edwin's house (bedroom with packing boxes, hallway, kitchen, porch), the back yard and the pine woods behind it,
  a hospital room and corridor, a car at the kerb.
- **Timeline**: week 1 move in - night 1 rustling - night 2 the window and the thump - morning the gash - hospital, the kidney
  gone - night 3 face to face, the photo, the run, the fall - hospital again, Edwin is dead - the house, the hallway, the bundle.
- **Twist**: the thing did not come for Mitch alone; it had been taking from him for nights, and it stayed for Edwin.

## 4. Kit r (new look, different from p, o, n, m, q)
- **Place and era**: a cheap American suburban house and a small hospital, present day.
- **Palette**: clinical **teal-green** and charcoal, with **sodium-amber** porch light; the single **red** accent is the camera's
  record light and the red EXIT sign in the hospital corridor.
- **Shapes**: stacked packing cartons, a bare mattress and a metal bed frame, vertical blinds, hospital bed rails, a boxy camera.
- **Camera habit**: the **bedside rise** - the camera starts low at floor or mattress level and rises with a slow tilt (smoothstep),
  so every room is first seen from the height of somebody lying down.
- **Chapter card**: a **hospital wristband** with "NIGHT n" printed in red on the band.
