# Story Bible & Full Scenario: The Seer of Possibilities

## 1. Story Bible

### Characters
- **Jack (Narrator / Protagonist)**: Noah Reed (`CAST3.noah`), quiet, reserved office analyst who avoids people. Weary, lonely, transformed over 12 years into a wealthy family man.
- **The Seer**: Ancient disembodied oracle, executed by a foolish king thousands of years ago. Exists as an omniscient voice and glowing crimson text on dark screens (`GHOST3.woman` / spectral crimson aura).
- **Allie**: Mira Vale (`CAST3.mira`), cheerful, radiant blonde coworker in cubicle 7B.
- **James Bentley**: Department manager (`CAST3.terence`), demanding, limping with a twisted ankle, later in a coma.
- **Donna**: Jack's devoted wife of 12 years (`CAST3.donna`), gentle, warm smile.
- **The Delivery Hulk**: 6'6" massive, muscular teenager with a Riago's Pizza cap, emotionally destroyed by a 12-year chain of trauma, brandishing a blade.
- **The Lottery Winner**: Burly construction worker (`CAST3.tom`) in Garmin's Liquor Store.

### Settings & Places
1. **Jack's Dark Study (Early)**: Modest apartment room, CRT monitor casting green/cyan glow, router blinking red, rain streaking the glass.
2. **Corporate Office Lobby**: 10-story high-rise, polished marble floor, elevator bank, heavy potted ficus plant against beige travertine wall.
3. **Office Cubicle Floor (7th floor)**: Fluorescent fixtures, cubicle partitions, desk phones, windows overlooking the intersection.
4. **City Intersection**: Wet asphalt, traffic lights, crumpled blue sedan crushed against an 18-wheeler, emergency flashers.
5. **Garmin's Liquor Store**: Neon signs, dusty glass shelves, counter, scratch-off slips.
6. **Jack & Donna's Rural Home**: Modern 2-story country residence, warm living room couch, glowing TV, pizza box on table, staircase leading to upstairs study.
7. **The Ancient Void / Memory**: Flashback to an ancient throne room with royal drapery and an easel holding a painted prophetic tapestry of red fate lines.

### Rules & Aesthetics
- **Engine**: v0.5 3D, 60 fps, flat-shaded low-poly meshes (`MeshStandardMaterial` with `flatShading: true`).
- **Palette**: Obsidian night & slate blue (`#0e1622`), CRT green/cyan phosphors, warm desk lamp amber (`#ffaa44`), and the signature red accent: **The Seer's glowing red screen text and router LED** (`#ff2b2b`).
- **Camera Style**: Creeping push-ins and slow orbits that feel like an invisible, all-knowing presence watching Jack from across the room.
- **Safety / Policy**: All violence is implied. Allie's crash is seen through distant wreckage, screaming sirens, and shocked onlookers. Donna's demise is heard as a muffled struggle downstairs while Jack is trapped at the computer. Zero gore or open wounds.

## 2. Full Scene-by-Scene Storyboard (Scenario)

### Act 0: The Hook & The Red Text
- **Scene 0.1 (fog/room)**: Over-the-shoulder shot of Jack seated at his desk. Solitaire cards frozen on screen. A blinking red LED on the router. Camera does a slow, ominous push-in.
- **Scene 0.2 (monitor detail)**: The cards vanish. Pure black glass. Red text bleeds across: *"Hi Jack, I need a favor from you."* Camera orbits slowly.
- **Scene 0.3 (house night)**: Exterior of Jack's dark house 12 years later. Dead branches swaying in the chill wind. Ominous shadow cast across the driveway.
- **Scene 0.4 (stairs/study)**: The modern home office. Crimson glow illuminating Jack's pale face. The closing message: *"Tonight, our masterpiece ends with you."*

### Act 1: The Potted Ficus
- **Scene 1.1 (foyer wide)**: Ten-story office lobby at 8:16 AM. Polished marble, elevator doors gleaming, large heavy potted plant centered between elevators.
- **Scene 1.2 (action waist)**: Jack checks his watch at 8:17 AM. Grabs the heavy clay pot and drags it exactly three inches out from the wall.
- **Scene 1.3 (elevator arrival)**: Elevators open. Morning crowd floods in. Jack slips into the car just as manager James Bentley arrives.

### Act 2: The Twisted Ankle
- **Scene 2.1 (cubicle waist)**: 7th-floor office cubicles. Jack chats with Allie at her desk. Morning light through office windows.
- **Scene 2.2 (limp entrance)**: Elevator doors slide open. James Bentley hobbles in loudly cursing, clutching his swollen right ankle.
- **Scene 2.3 (Jack realization)**: Close shot of Jack sinking into his chair in stunned silence.
- **Scene 2.4 (evening monitor)**: Jack returns home, powers on computer. Screen goes black: *"How was your day, Jack? This chain isn't done playing out."*

### Act 3: The Intersection
- **Scene 3.1 (hallway 3pm)**: James Bentley limping heavily, pleading with Allie to drive his car to the medical clinic. Allie smiles and waves goodbye to Jack.
- **Scene 3.2 (window shock)**: Loud blast of an air horn followed by the sickening crunch of metal. Office workers rush to the high-rise windows.
- **Scene 3.3 (street aftermath)**: Street level. Flashing red lights, crowd held back. A massive truck broadside into James' sedan. Sirens wailing.
- **Scene 3.4 (Jack panic)**: Jack walking back in daze, tears and horror.

### Act 4: The Faustian Compact
- **Scene 4.1 (monitor debate)**: Jack staring at the monitor. Text explains Allie's unfaithfulness and how the tragedy averted years of ruin.
- **Scene 4.2 (monitor smash)**: Jack screams, tossing the CRT onto the floor where it sparks out.
- **Scene 4.3 (office promotion)**: Director Diego Salbara offers Jack the division head promotion with huge salary increase.
- **Scene 4.4 (the seer speaks)**: New monitor set up. The Seer formally introduces herself as an ancient bodiless oracle of human manipulation: *"You will be my paintbrush, and humanity my canvas."*
- **Scene 4.5 (the reply)**: Jack's fingers hesitate over the keyboard, then type: *"What do you want me to do next?"*

### Act 5: The Domino Web
- **Scene 5.1 (liquor store)**: Garmin's Liquor Store counter. Jack tells the burly construction worker: *"Twenty-seven."*
- **Scene 5.2 (grocery reunion)**: Two weeks later, the smiling man hands Jack a $10,000 cashier's check.
- **Scene 5.3 (seer truth)**: Monitor reveals the dark future: the man will lose every cent, beat a young boy, and his family will abandon him.
- **Scene 5.4 (parking spot 43)**: Jack parking his car in spot 43 at dusk. A chain reaction involving 25 souls that leads Donna directly into his life.

### Act 6: Twelve Years of Prosperity
- **Scene 6.1 (rural estate)**: Beautiful country home, green lawn, autumnal trees, evening twilight.
- **Scene 6.2 (desk phone)**: Jack sitting in his study, making the 2-minute phone call to Riago's Pizza, letting it ring 3 times, hanging up.
- **Scene 6.3 (front door knock)**: 6'6" muscular, sullen teenager in a pizza cap thrusts a box into Jack's hands. No tip given. The boy glares with cold rage.
- **Scene 6.4 (living room peace)**: Jack and Donna enjoying pizza together on the sofa. Donna drifts off to sleep under a warm blanket.

### Act 7: The Masterpiece Revealed
- **Scene 7.1 (study night)**: Jack returns to the glowing screen at 11:30 PM.
- **Scene 7.2 (the revelation)**: Red text unfolds the horrifying truth: every favor over 12 years was engineered to shape the delivery boy into a murderous monster.
- **Scene 7.3 (the noise)**: A muffled gasp and sickening silence from downstairs.
- **Scene 7.4 (voice in mind)**: A soothing, feminine voice whispers directly inside Jack's mind, freezing him in place.

### Act 8: The Final Whisper
- **Scene 8.1 (doorway shadow)**: The massive frame of the boy appears in the study doorway, silhouette clutching a glinting knife.
- **Scene 8.2 (screen question)**: Monitor flashes: *"Don't you have something to say to me, Jack?"*
- **Scene 8.3 (acceptance)**: Tears running down Jack's cheeks, blending grief with profound gratitude. He mouths: *"Thank you."*
- **Scene 8.4 (end card)**: The final question appears in crisp bold text over the dark ambient room: *"Would you trade eighty-five bitter years for twelve perfect ones?"*
