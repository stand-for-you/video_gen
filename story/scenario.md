# Gateway of the Mind — Story Bible & Scenario

## 1. Story Bible
- **Setting & Era**: 1983, an undisclosed underground research laboratory and surgical sensory deprivation facility. Cold concrete, stainless steel, cathode ray monitors, reel-to-reel tape recorders, one-way surveillance glass.
- **Story Kit**: `Kit q` — `Facility83` / `Gateway`. Muted concrete grey (`#2e3438`), monitor phosphor green (`#5a7a60`), sterile linoleum; single dark red accent = recording LED and alarm indicator (`#8a1a1a`).
- **Characters**:
  - **Subject 83 (Elderly Volunteer)**: Elderly volunteer with nothing left to live for. Severe sensory isolation: numb, deaf, blind. Pale, hospital gown, vacant blind gaze, tears streaming.
  - **Dr. Reed (Lead Scientist)**: Deeply pious, obsessive researcher, white lab coat, red wrist thread, clipboard, observing through one-way glass.
  - **Research Team**: Pious scientists in lab coats, monitoring audio tapes, fleeing when private secrets of dead relatives are spoken.
  - **The Apparitions / Dead**: Ghost Woman (deceased wife), hollow-eyed channel ghosts representing the swarm of deceased relatives in his mind.
- **Twist / Climax**: Instead of reaching God, severing the sensory barrier unleashed an inescapable swarm of deceased souls. In his final breath, the blind subject locks eyes with the scientist and delivers the terrifying verdict: "I have spoken with God... and he has abandoned us."
