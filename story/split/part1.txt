[32.12-40.16] Instead, something else found him in the dark: hundreds of dead voices that refused to leave.

## The Experiment
[51.39-60.41] In nineteen eighty-three, a team of deeply religious scientists gathered at an undisclosed research facility.
[60.70-73.04] They had theorized that our physical senses cloud our awareness of eternity, and without them, a human could actually contact God by thought alone.
[73.49-81.12] An elderly man who claimed he had nothing left to live for was the only test subject to volunteer.
[81.42-93.15] To purge him of all stimuli, surgeons performed a complex operation, severing every sensory nerve connection leading into his brain.
[93.60-102.57] Although the man retained full muscular function, he could no longer see, hear, taste, smell, or feel.
[102.87-109.82] With no possible way to sense the outside world, he was completely alone with his thoughts.
[110.27-117.54] This story is inspired by Gateway of the Mind, as published on the Creepypasta Wiki.
## The Voices
[118.14-129.12] Scientists monitored him through one-way glass as he spoke aloud in jumbled, slurred sentences that he could not even hear.
[129.42-138.32] After four days, the man claimed he was hearing hushed, unintelligible voices whispering inside his head.
[138.77-146.99] Assuming it was merely an onset of psychosis, the researchers paid little attention to his concerns.
[147.29-156.15] Two days later, the subject began to weep, crying out that he could hear his dead wife conversing with him.
[156.60-164.22] He claimed he could communicate back through his thoughts, whispering: [subject]"She is right here with me."
[164.68-174.68] The scientists were skeptical, until the man began reciting the private names and secrets of their own dead relatives.
[174.98-187.70] He repeated personal truths that only their deceased spouses and parents could have known, driving a sizable portion of the team to abandon the facility.
